Thermal Printer Setup Guide

Follow these 4 settings in your browser's Print Dialog (Ctrl+P / Cmd+P) for 100% sharp, zero-margin labels:

  1. Destination: Select your thermal printer (e.g. Zebra, Rollo, Munbyn, Brother, Phomemo).
  2. Paper Size: Choose the paper dimension matching your roll (e.g. 50×25mm or 2"×1").
  3. Margins: Set to "None" or "Custom (0mm)" to prevent extra blank labels.
  4. Scale: Set to 100% (Default) or "Fit to printable area".
  5. Options: Enable "Background Graphics".
📱 Mobile Note: You can draft SKUs, preview layouts, and generate batches on mobile. For physical thermal roll printing, connect via Bluetooth/AirPrint or open on your desktop thermal workstation.
