# iconic the salon — Bridal Studio & Beauty Parlour Website

An elegant, bridal-focused website and consultation booking portal for iconic the salon, voted #1 Salon in Bhavnagar on ThreeBestRated.in, located in Kaliyabid.

---

## 🚀 Key Features

- **Bridal Token Palette**: Dark plum-black (`#1C1218`), blush-white (`#FDF6F0`), dusty rose (`#D4617A`), deep berry (`#8B2D4A`), and nude peach (`#F0C4B4`).
- **Typography**: Libre Baskerville 700 display serif headlines and Nunito Sans body typography.
- **3-Tier Bridal Packages**: Silver, Gold (with "Most Popular" ribbon badge), and Platinum tiers prioritized before general salon services.
- **2x4 Beauty Services Grid**: Haircut, facial, party makeup, hair spa, gel nails, waxing, and eyelash extensions.
- **Portfolio & Testimonial Proof**: Real bride testimonials with wedding dates and hoverable portfolio gallery.
- **WhatsApp Bridal Consultation**: Form validation with pre-filled WhatsApp link generation.

---

## 🛠️ Technology Stack

- **Markup**: HTML5 with Schema.org local business markup
- **Styling**: CSS3 (Libre Baskerville & Nunito Sans Google Fonts, dusty rose highlights)
- **Logic**: Vanilla JavaScript (ES6+ package pre-fill & WhatsApp booking)
