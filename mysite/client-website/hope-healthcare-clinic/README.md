# Hope Health Care Clinic — Skin & Laser Centre Website

An editorial, patient-reassuring website and skin consultation portal for Hope Health Care Clinic, located in Bhavnagar, Gujarat.

---

## 🚀 Key Features

- **Skin Token Palette**: Warm white (`#F9F5F0`), slate blue (`#5B7FA6`), terracotta skin accent (`#C97E5E`), and dark strip `#1E2A3A`.
- **Typography**: DM Serif Display for sophisticated cosmetic headlines and DM Sans for body typography.
- **Tabbed Treatment Catalog**: Skin Care, Hair Restoration (PRP), Laser Hair Removal, and Chemical Peels.
- **Interactive Before & After Result Slider**: Dynamic CSS clip range slider comparing treatment transformations.
- **3-Step Patient Process**: 01 Consult → 02 Customised Plan → 03 Treatment.
- **WhatsApp Skin Inquiry**: Dropdown concern selector with instant WhatsApp messaging.

---

## 🛠️ Technology Stack

- **Markup**: HTML5 with semantic tags & local business schema
- **Styling**: CSS3 (DM Serif Display & DM Sans Google Fonts, terracotta highlights)
- **Logic**: Vanilla JavaScript (ES6+ interactive range slider & consultation handler)
