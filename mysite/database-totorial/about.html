About Database Design Masterclass

An interactive educational platform designed to teach real-world production database architecture through actual system design documents.

🎯 Platform Mission

Most database tutorials only teach basic SQL syntax (`SELECT`, `INSERT`, `UPDATE`). However, in real production applications, the hardest technical challenges are deciding how to structure entities, where to place Foreign Keys, how to handle concurrency, and why specific design choices are made.

Database Design Masterclass fills this gap by breaking down 11+ production system architectures (E-Commerce, Chat Applications, Hospitals, SaaS Platforms, Booking Systems, Real Estate, DMS, and more) into structured, interactive courses.

📚 The Learning Methodology

WHY Understand the business problem and real-world workflows first.
WHAT Identify business modules and core relational entities.
HOW Design ER relationships (1:1, 1:N, N:M) and define tables.
PITFALLS Study ❌ Bad vs ✅ Better architectural comparisons and avoid beginner mistakes.
