What is a GUID / UUID?

A GUID (Globally Unique Identifier), also known as a UUID (Universally Unique Identifier), is a 128-bit integer number used to uniquely identify information in computer systems. Standard UUIDs are composed of 32 hexadecimal digits displayed in 5 groups separated by hyphens (e.g. 8-4-4-4-12 format).
